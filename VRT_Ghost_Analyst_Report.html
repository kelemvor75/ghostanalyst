1  |  PRICE ACTION & REVENUE TRAJECTORY

VRT has surged from ~$100 to $251 over 9 months — a 151% move driven by AI data center demand for power and cooling infrastructure. The stock peaked at $275+ in January before pulling back amid the broader tech selloff. Revenue acceleration from $2.04B to $2.88B in four quarters with expanding margins from 33.7% to 38.9% demonstrates both volume growth and pricing power.

The pullback from $275 to $251 has narrowed the gap to analyst consensus ($265), but the fundamental trajectory — 46% EPS growth in FY2026 accelerating through FY2027 — suggests targets will be revised higher. Vertiv is the FIX/STRL of the power and cooling layer: not the GPU that gets the headlines, but the infrastructure that makes GPUs functional.

2  |  BUSINESS MODEL & COMPETITIVE POSITION
Revenue Architecture
Americas (~50%): UPS systems, precision cooling, power distribution for U.S. and Latin American data centers. Strongest growth region driven by hyperscaler buildout.
Asia Pacific (~25%): Growing rapidly with data center expansion in India, Southeast Asia, and Japan. Emerging AI infrastructure demand.
EMEA (~25%): European data center market. Growing but constrained by power grid limitations in key markets.

Vertiv’s competitive advantage is the combination of hardware and services for mission-critical power and cooling. A data center UPS failure means servers go dark. A cooling failure means GPUs throttle or overheat. These are not commodity products — they require engineering, custom configuration, and ongoing maintenance. Vertiv’s installed base of millions of units generates recurring service revenue that grows with the fleet, providing visibility and margin expansion.

The liquid cooling transition is Vertiv’s next growth vector. As GPU thermal design power (TDP) exceeds 700W per chip with NVIDIA Blackwell, air cooling becomes physically insufficient. Vertiv’s liquid cooling solutions — including direct-to-chip and rear-door heat exchangers — address this thermal wall. The transition from air to liquid cooling is not optional; it’s physics. And Vertiv is the market leader in data center thermal management.
