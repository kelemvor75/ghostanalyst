2  |  BUSINESS MODEL & COMPETITIVE POSITION
Revenue Architecture
Product Revenue (~80%): Ethernet switches (7000 series for data centers, campus switches) and routers. The 7800R AI spine switches are the primary growth driver — purpose-built for AI/ML cluster networking with 800G ports and RDMA support. Growing 35%+ on AI cluster deployments.
Service & Support (~20%): Post-sale technical support, software subscriptions, and professional services. High-margin recurring revenue that grows with the installed base. Renewal rates exceed 90%.

Arista’s competitive moat is EOS (Extensible Operating System), a software-defined networking platform that Cisco has been unable to replicate despite a decade of trying. EOS runs on merchant silicon from Broadcom and Intel, allowing Arista to adopt the latest chip technology faster than Cisco’s proprietary ASIC approach. The result is switches that are faster, more reliable, and cheaper per port than Cisco’s equivalent products. In AI networking, this advantage compounds because AI clusters require deterministic, low-latency networking that EOS delivers through RDMA and RoCEv2 support.

The AI networking TAM is growing faster than AI compute because network complexity scales with the square of the number of endpoints. A 1,000-GPU cluster needs exponentially more network bandwidth than a 100-GPU cluster due to all-reduce operations in distributed training. Arista’s 7800R spine switches with 800G ports are the industry standard for this topology. As clusters scale to 100K+ GPUs, the networking TAM could exceed the GPU TAM — a dynamic that most investors haven’t fully priced.
