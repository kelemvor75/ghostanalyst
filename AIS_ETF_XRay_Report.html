AIS earns an X-Ray Score of B (76/100). It is, on the evidence, a well-built and genuinely active AI-infrastructure fund: thesis-pure (88% tech, minimal drift), differentiated from cheap semi-beta (only ~26–31% overlap), holding an elite-quality basket (look-through ROE 57%, Altman-Z 20, 18% forward growth) that has delivered exceptional risk-adjusted returns (+241%, Sharpe 1.88, +57% alpha).

But the wrapper is where the grade comes down, and honestly so: a 0.75% fee (D on cost), a 40%-vol, -33%-drawdown, 5x-DeepSeek-beta risk profile (C+ on risk), and a bottom-up look-through showing the basket trading above its own price targets with net insider selling across 55% of the book — a portfolio priced for continued perfection. The archetype is a satellite, not a core: a high-octane way to express an explicit, time-bounded bullish view on the global AI-hardware supercycle, sized small and watched closely.

Bottom line — who it's for, and who it isn't. For an investor without AI-infrastructure exposure who wants one professionally-managed, genuinely-global ticket on the theme and accepts the volatility and fee: a credible satellite holding (X-Ray B). For you specifically (already running a concentrated AI-infra book): redundant — ~26% overlaps what you own, the fee buys you mainly foreign exposure obtainable more cheaply direct, and you have outperformed the fund. Most useful to you as an external benchmark for your own selection alpha. Either way, the look-through verdict on the underlying basket — elite quality, fully valued, late-cycle positioning — is the durable takeaway.
