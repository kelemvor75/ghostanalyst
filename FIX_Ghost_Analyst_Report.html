1  |  PRICE ACTION & REVENUE TRAJECTORY

FIX has been a steady compounder, rising from ~$700 in July 2025 to $1,379 today — a 97% move driven by the AI data center buildout. Revenue has accelerated from $1.83B to $2.65B in four quarters, with each quarter setting a new record. The stock trades with low volatility by semiconductor standards but the move has been persistent and strong.

The AI data center construction pipeline provides multi-year visibility that traditional commercial construction lacks. Hyperscalers plan capacity 2-3 years ahead, and MEP contracts are awarded 12-18 months before facility completion. This means Comfort Systems’ backlog today reflects demand that extends well into 2027-2028.

2  |  BUSINESS MODEL & COMPETITIVE POSITION
Revenue Architecture
Mechanical Services (~50%): HVAC installation and maintenance for data centers, hospitals, manufacturing. Every building with servers needs precision climate control.
Electrical Services (~30%): Electrical systems installation for commercial and industrial facilities. Growing as data center electrical loads scale with GPU density.
Building Services (~20%): Plumbing, fire protection, building automation. Complementary services that allow full-building MEP contracts.

Comfort Systems’ acquisition-driven growth model is the strategic moat. The company acquires best-in-class regional MEP contractors, integrates them into its national platform, and leverages cross-selling to win larger, more complex projects. This roll-up strategy has driven 20+ years of compounding returns. The AI data center wave is accelerating this — hyperscalers want national contractors who can deliver consistent quality across multiple sites.

Margin expansion from 22% to 25.5% over four quarters reflects a favorable mix shift toward complex data center projects. Data center HVAC systems are more technically demanding — requiring precision cooling for GPU racks running at 40+ kW per unit — than standard commercial installations, and command higher margins.
