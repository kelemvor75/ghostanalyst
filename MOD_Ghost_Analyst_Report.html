1  |  PRICE ACTION & REVENUE TRAJECTORY

MOD has rallied from $50 to $223 over the past 12 months — a 346% move as the market recognized data center thermal management as a critical AI infrastructure category. The stock’s trajectory closely mirrors VRT (Vertiv) but with a smaller market cap ($12B vs $99B) and less analyst coverage, suggesting the re-rating has further to run.

Revenue has accelerated every quarter from $620M to $810M with margins holding steady in the 22-25% range. The Q3 FY2026 EPS of -$0.90 reflects one-time items, not operational deterioration — the underlying business is growing 31% with consistent profitability. DA Davidson has maintained Buy and raised targets three times in four months, signaling confidence in the trajectory.

2  |  BUSINESS MODEL & COMPETITIVE POSITION
Revenue Architecture
Climate Solutions (~60%): Data center cooling systems (precision air handling, liquid cooling, rear-door heat exchangers), commercial HVAC, and building automation. The AI data center growth engine. Growing 35%+.
Performance Technologies (~40%): EV battery thermal management, automotive heat exchangers, industrial cooling, and off-highway vehicle thermal systems. Growing with EV adoption and electrification trends.

Modine’s competitive positioning in data center cooling is built on engineering depth in thermal management — the same physics that applies to cooling a car engine also applies to cooling a GPU rack. The company has pivoted its legacy automotive thermal expertise toward data center applications where the cooling challenge is more complex and the margins are higher. This pivot is reflected in the revenue mix shift toward Climate Solutions, which now represents 60% of revenue.

The comparison with Vertiv is instructive. VRT is the market leader at $99B market cap; MOD is the challenger at $12B. Both benefit from the same GPU density → thermal management demand driver. But MOD’s smaller size means each data center cooling win has a proportionally larger impact on growth. At 30x FY27E vs Vertiv’s 31x, MOD is trading at parity despite having higher EPS growth (51% vs 31%) — suggesting relative value if the thesis plays out.
