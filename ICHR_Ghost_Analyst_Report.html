1  |  PRICE ACTION & REVENUE TRAJECTORY

ICHR has rallied 398% from its 52-week low of $13.12 to $65, making it one of the strongest performers in the semiconductor equipment space in 2026. The rally began in late 2025 as the WFE (wafer fabrication equipment) spending cycle inflected from downturn to recovery, driven by AI chip demand, high-bandwidth memory buildouts, and advanced packaging investments. Needham upgraded to Buy on January 20 with a $36 target; Craig-Hallum raised to $36 in January; Stifel upgraded Hold to Buy on March 17.

The stock now trades near the updated $76.71 analyst consensus after post-Q1 target raises — Needham’s latest target is $76, and the high estimate is $76. The Q1 earnings beat on May 4 (non-GAAP EPS $0.15 vs $0.13, revenue $256M vs $251M) was followed by Q2 guidance that crushed expectations ($0.25–$0.35 EPS vs $0.20 consensus, $290–$310M revenue). The stock briefly dipped 2.8% post-earnings on profit-taking, recovered to $77 by May 11, then dropped 15% on May 18 when Ichor filed a $200M at-the-market equity offering alongside an automatic shelf registration, reflecting the pattern of a stock where fundamentals are outrunning analyst estimates. Mexico capacity expansion is ahead of schedule, positioning Ichor for tariff-resilient growth.

2  |  BUSINESS MODEL & COMPETITIVE POSITION
Revenue Architecture

Gas Delivery Subsystems (~55%): Precision systems that deliver, monitor, and control specialized gases used in semiconductor etch and deposition processes. Every advanced node fab tool requires these subsystems. Content per tool is increasing with node complexity.

Chemical Delivery Subsystems (~25%): Systems that blend and dispense reactive liquid chemistries for CMP (chemical-mechanical planarization), electroplating, and cleaning. Growing with advanced packaging demand.

Precision Components & Weldments (~20%): Precision-machined components, e-beam and laser welded assemblies, vacuum brazing, and surface treatments. High-value, high-margin products that are expanding as Ichor vertically integrates.

Ichor occupies a critical but often overlooked niche in the semiconductor supply chain. It doesn’t make the chips or even the tools — it makes the subsystems inside the tools. When Lam Research builds an etch tool or Applied Materials builds a deposition system, the fluid delivery subsystem that manages gas and chemical flow comes from Ichor. This makes Ichor a pure picks-and-shovels play on semiconductor capex: every dollar spent on WFE generates revenue for Ichor regardless of which chipmaker or which tool vendor wins.

The business model is inherently cyclical — seven quarters of GAAP losses during the 2024-2025 downcycle prove that. But the leverage works both ways: as WFE spending inflects upward, Ichor’s revenue and margins expand rapidly because the cost structure is largely fixed. The Q3 2025 trough (5% gross margin) to Q2 2026E (13–14%) represents an 800+ basis point expansion in three quarters. Customer concentration is a real risk — Lam Research and Applied Materials together represent the majority of revenue — but these are the two largest WFE companies in the world, and both are growing.

9  |  WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR MIND

We would downgrade from BUY if:

WFE spending growth decelerates below 5%. Would signal the equipment cycle is peaking, not early innings.

Lam Research or Applied Materials announce in-sourcing fluid delivery. Would be existential for Ichor’s core business.

Gross margins stall below 14% for two quarters. Would indicate operating leverage isn’t materializing.

Revenue falls below $240M for two consecutive quarters. Would indicate the recovery was a head fake.

10  |  PRICE TARGET & CONCLUSION
Price Target: $80  |  Rating: BUY

Our $80 target represents 23% upside from $67.64, derived from 28x FY2027E non-GAAP EPS of $2.61 plus a premium for the early-cycle positioning and secular AI capex tailwind.

Ichor is the purest picks-and-shovels play on semiconductor capex in our coverage. It doesn’t matter whether Intel or TSMC wins the foundry race, whether NVIDIA or AMD wins the GPU race, or whether etch or deposition grows faster — Ichor’s subsystems go into every tool from every vendor in every fab. Revenue is inflecting ($224M → $256M → $300M guided), margins are expanding (5% → 13% → 14% guided), and the WFE cycle is in its early innings with 15–20% growth expected through 2027.

The 398% rally from $13 creates legitimate concern about chasing, and the $200M ATM filed May 18 creates near-term dilution pressure and explains the insider selling. But the consensus is lagging — Q2 guidance crushed estimates by 55–75%, and targets will reset higher. At 25x FY27E with near-100% EPS growth, a sub-$3B market cap, and leverage to the most powerful capex cycle in semiconductor history, ICHR offers the kind of small-cap upside that our large-cap coverage cannot.
