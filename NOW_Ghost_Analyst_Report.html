1  |  PRICE ACTION & REVENUE TRAJECTORY

NOW has been crushed from $190 to $83 — a 56% decline that is the most severe pullback in the stock’s history as a public company. The selloff is entirely macro-driven: the Iran conflict has triggered a growth-to-value rotation, enterprise software budgets are being questioned, and the market is pricing in a recession scenario that would slow IT spending. Revenue tells a different story — $2.80B → $2.96B → $3.09B → $3.21B → $3.41B → $3.57B, six consecutive quarters of acceleration.

The 131% gap to analyst consensus ($192) is the widest in enterprise software. Four major banks maintained Buy ratings in April alone (Goldman, BTIG, Stifel, Wells Fargo), signaling that institutional conviction hasn’t wavered despite the price action. NOW’s subscription revenue (97% of total) with 95%+ renewal rates means revenue doesn’t disappear even in a downturn — it just grows slower. This is a durable business model being priced for a scenario that would require enterprise IT departments to stop functioning.

2  |  BUSINESS MODEL & COMPETITIVE POSITION
Revenue Architecture
IT Service Management (ITSM, ~40%): The core platform. Incident management, problem management, change management, and asset management for IT departments. Installed in virtually every Fortune 500 IT organization. The ‘land’ product.
IT Operations Management (ITOM, ~20%): Cloud management, infrastructure discovery, event management. Extends the ITSM platform into operational monitoring. Growing with cloud complexity.
HR Service Delivery & Other Workflows (~25%): Employee onboarding, case management, knowledge management applied to HR, legal, facilities, and finance. The ‘expand’ product that drives net expansion rates above 125%.
Security Operations & Now Assist AI (~15%): Security incident response, vulnerability management, and AI-powered workflow automation. Now Assist embeds generative AI into every workflow on the platform. The newest growth vector.

ServiceNow’s moat is workflow ownership. Once an enterprise deploys ServiceNow for IT service management, every subsequent workflow (HR, security, customer service, finance) is easier to build on the same platform than on a competing one. The data model, user permissions, integration framework, and training investment all compound. This creates switching costs measured in years and millions of dollars — which is why renewal rates exceed 95% and net expansion rates exceed 125%.

The Now Assist AI strategy is the most natural AI monetization in enterprise software. Unlike companies that need to build AI products from scratch, ServiceNow already owns the workflow layer where AI agents execute. An AI agent that can automatically resolve IT tickets, process HR requests, or triage security incidents has immediate, measurable ROI for the customer. ServiceNow charges a premium for AI capabilities layered onto existing subscriptions — a frictionless upsell to 8,100+ existing customers.
