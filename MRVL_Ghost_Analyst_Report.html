1  |  PRICE ACTION & REVENUE TRAJECTORY

MRVL peaked at $130 in January before pulling back to $109 amid the broader semiconductor selloff. The NVIDIA $2B investment on March 31 provided a floor, signaling that the most important GPU company on earth views Marvell as a critical partner. Revenue has nearly doubled in five quarters: $1.16B → $1.90B → $2.01B → $2.07B → $2.22B, with each quarter setting a new record.

The 16% pullback from highs has created a valuation gap: MRVL at 20x FY28E with 41% EPS growth and the NVIDIA partnership compares favorably to AMD (20x, 34% growth) and Broadcom (22x, 20% growth). BofA’s upgrade from Neutral to Buy on March 6 removed the last major bearish voice, leaving 36 Buy vs 7 Hold and zero Sells. The stock is coiled for re-rating on the next positive catalyst.

2  |  BUSINESS MODEL & COMPETITIVE POSITION
Revenue Architecture
Data Center (~70%, fastest growing): Custom AI accelerator interconnects (Amazon, Google, Microsoft), data center networking switches and PHYs, optical DSPs for 800G/1.6T transceivers, and silicon photonics co-developed with NVIDIA. This segment is growing 40%+ and is the core of the AI thesis.
Enterprise Networking (~15%): Ethernet switches, PHYs, and controllers for enterprise campus and data center networks. Stable, mature. Benefits from Wi-Fi 7 and campus network upgrades.
Carrier Infrastructure (~8%): 5G baseband processors and transport networking. Declining as 5G capex normalizes. Not a growth driver.
Consumer (~7%): Storage controllers for SSDs and HDDs, gaming console chips. Mature, stable. Provides diversification.

Marvell occupies a unique position in the AI infrastructure stack: it designs the custom silicon and networking fabric that connects AI accelerators. While NVIDIA dominates the GPU itself, the data center network that links thousands of GPUs requires custom ASICs, optical DSPs, and switch silicon that Marvell specializes in. The $2B NVIDIA investment is explicitly for NVLink Fusion — the next-generation interconnect that will link GPU clusters across racks and data centers using Marvell’s silicon photonics technology.

The custom silicon business is Marvell’s most differentiated offering. Hyperscalers like Amazon (Trainium), Google (TPU), and Microsoft design their own AI accelerators but rely on Marvell to design the surrounding interconnect and networking silicon. This positions Marvell as the ‘Switzerland of AI silicon’ — a trusted partner to every hyperscaler regardless of whether they use NVIDIA GPUs or their own custom chips. The switching costs are measured in years of co-design, not dollars.

9  |  WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR MIND

We would downgrade from BUY if:

Data center revenue growth decelerates below 20%. Would signal AI networking and custom silicon demand is peaking.
A major hyperscaler cancels or delays a custom silicon program. Would directly impact the highest-margin revenue stream.
Broadcom wins NVIDIA’s primary networking silicon sockets. Would undermine the competitive positioning the NVIDIA investment validates.
Gross margins fall below 48%. Would indicate custom silicon economics are less favorable than expected.
