Investment Thesis
Absolute Monopoly on the Future of Chipmaking. ASML is the sole manufacturer of EUV lithography systems — the machines required to produce every leading-edge semiconductor chip on earth.[1] There is no alternative supplier, no viable competing technology, and no path around EUV for chips below 7nm. Every NVIDIA GPU, every Apple processor, every AMD chip depends on ASML machines.
Record Backlog Provides Multi-Year Visibility. ASML ended 2025 with a $42.7B backlog, and Q4 2025 bookings of $14.5B smashed expectations of $6.9B — a record quarter.[2] SK Hynix placed a $7.97B order for EUV tools in March 2026.[3] This is a capacity-constrained monopoly rationing supply, not a cyclical company hoping for orders.
High NA EUV Is the Next Growth Vector. ASML shipped its first High NA (0.55 NA) EUV systems in Q4 2025 at approximately $385M per unit — roughly 5x the price of standard EUV tools.[4] High NA is required for sub-2nm chip manufacturing starting in 2026-2027. Intel, TSMC, and Samsung have all placed orders.

2  |  BUSINESS MODEL & MOAT
Revenue Architecture
Systems Revenue (~75%): EUV systems ($12.8B in 2025, 48 units shipped, +39% YoY) and DUV systems ($13.2B). EUV is the growth engine — ASPs are rising as High NA enters the mix at ~$385M/unit versus ~$80M for standard EUV.[4]
Installed Base Management (~25%): Service, upgrades, and spare parts for ~5,600 lithography systems deployed globally. This segment grew 26% YoY to $9.0B — high-margin, recurring, and growing.[5]
Moat — The Deepest in Technology

ASML's moat is categorically different from any other company in our coverage universe. Building an EUV lithography system requires integrating components from Zeiss (optics), Cymer/ASML (light source), and ASML (systems integration), with tolerances measured in single-digit nanometers across a machine the size of a bus. China has spent tens of billions attempting to develop domestic alternatives and has not produced a functional EUV system. The estimated timeline for a credible competitor is 10-15 years at minimum.

What's Priced In

At $1,254 and ~26x forward earnings, ASML trades at a modest premium to the semiconductor equipment peer group (TEL ~24x, KLA ~28x). Given the monopoly position and backlog visibility, this premium is arguably too small. The market is pricing in roughly $35 in forward EPS — close to our base case — suggesting limited downside if earnings simply meet expectations, with meaningful upside if bookings continue to surprise.

Roger Dassen, CFO: Veteran ASML executive who has overseen financial scaling from $18.6B (2021) to $35.9B (2025). Described Q4 2025 as a "record quarter for orders." Conservative guidance track record — ASML typically guides to the low end and delivers at the high end.[2]

9  |  WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR MIND

We would downgrade from Buy to Hold if:

Bookings fall below $6.6B for two consecutive quarters. Would signal demand is normalizing, not just lumpy timing.
Gross margins drop below 50%. Would indicate pricing pressure or cost overruns on High NA.
China successfully demonstrates a sub-20nm lithography system. Even a crude one would signal the monopoly has an expiration date.
TSMC or Samsung defer 2nm timelines by 12+ months. Would reduce near-term EUV demand and push High NA revenue out.
