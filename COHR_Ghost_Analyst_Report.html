2  |  BUSINESS MODEL & COMPETITIVE POSITION
Revenue Architecture
Networking / Datacom (~45%, fastest growing): Optical transceivers (800G shipping, 1.6T in development), silicon photonics modules, coherent DSP modules for AI data center interconnects. This segment is directly tied to GPU cluster scaling — every rack of GPUs needs optical connections to the network fabric. Growth rate exceeds 30% YoY.
Materials (~30%): Engineered materials and optoelectronic components — semiconductor laser components, infrared optics, precision optics for industrial and defense applications. Stable, predictable revenue base.
Lasers (~25%): Industrial and scientific lasers for manufacturing, materials processing, and life sciences. Mature business providing stable cash flow that funds datacom R&D.
Why Optical Transceivers Are a Structural Growth Market

Inside an AI data center, GPUs communicate through optical transceivers. As model sizes grow (GPT-5 may require 50,000+ GPUs training simultaneously), the bandwidth required between GPUs grows exponentially. Electrical connections hit physical limits above 400G — beyond that, optical is required. The transition from 400G to 800G to 1.6T transceivers is not optional; it's physics. Coherent, alongside Lumentum and Broadcom, is one of only three companies with the technology and manufacturing scale to supply these components at the volumes hyperscalers require.
