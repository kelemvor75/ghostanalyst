2  |  OPTIMUS: THE THESIS THAT CHANGES EVERYTHING
Where Optimus Stands (March 2026)

Tesla commenced Gen 3 production at Fremont in January 2026 and has deployed over 1,000 units.[1] However, Musk confirmed on the Q4 2025 earnings call that these robots are not yet doing "useful work" — they are deployed for learning and data collection.[9]

Gen 3 represents a genuine engineering leap: 50 actuators across the hands, 22 degrees of freedom, tendon-driven dexterity approaching human capability.[10] Videos show fluid bipedal locomotion including jogging.

3  |  FSD: CLOSER THAN THE MARKET THINKS

FSD v14.2 incorporates reasoning models — AI that understands context and predicts behavior, not just object recognition.[3] Tesla's safety data shows 7x fewer major/minor collisions with FSD engaged across 6.5+ billion miles.[4]

European FSD approval (UN R-171) is expected approximately April 10, 2026, with EU-wide rollout planned for summer 2026.[5] China FSD deployment is targeted for mid-2026. FSD switched to subscription-only in February 2026, building recurring revenue.

The AI5 inference chip, confirmed for late 2026, delivers 50x performance over AI4 — accelerating both FSD and Optimus.[6]

4  |  AUTO & ENERGY BUSINESS
Automotive (~73% of revenue) — Declining

FY25 auto revenue of $69.5B was down 10% YoY.[7] BYD has overtaken Tesla as the world's largest BEV manufacturer. Q4 2025 gross margin of 20.1% (highest in two years) suggests the margin trough is behind.[11] The lower-cost model and Model Y refresh are volume catalysts, but Chinese competitive pressure is structural.

Energy Storage (~13% of revenue) — Growing Rapidly

Revenue of $12.8B (+27%) with 46.7 GWh deployed in 2025.[7] Megapack demand accelerating. This is the most predictable growth vector.

11  |  PRICE TARGET & CONCLUSION
Price Target: $305  |  Rating: HOLD

Our $305 target represents 14% downside from the March 31 close of $355.28.

Let us be direct about why this is a HOLD and not a BUY: Tesla's revenue declined 3% in FY2025. Our quantitative model — validated across 3,221 observations over 8 years with 100% out-of-sample consistency — explicitly excludes companies with declining revenue. We don't override the model because we like the story. The model exists precisely to prevent narrative from overriding data.

That said, we don't rate Tesla SELL because the optionality is real. Optimus has 1,000+ units deployed. FSD v14 is the most advanced consumer autonomous driving system in the world. Energy storage grew 27%. And Musk has delivered on impossible ambitions more times than any entrepreneur in history. These are facts, not speculation.

The path to a BUY upgrade is clear: FY2026 revenue growth must turn positive. If Q1 + Q2 2026 revenue exceeds the year-ago periods — which consensus estimates suggest it will — the fundamental gate flips and we re-score. That earnings report on April 21 could be the catalyst. Until then, the model says wait.
