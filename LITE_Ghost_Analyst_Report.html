2  |  BUSINESS MODEL & COMPETITIVE POSITION
Revenue Architecture
Cloud & Networking (~65%, fastest growing): 800G optical transceivers, coherent receivers, ROADMs, and wavelength-selective switches for AI data center interconnects. This is the growth engine — directly tied to GPU cluster scaling. Every new AI rack needs Lumentum optics.
Industrial Tech (~20%): 3D sensing (Face ID in iPhones), LIDAR components, fiber lasers for manufacturing. Stable, diversified.
Telecom (~15%): Long-haul and metro optical components for carrier networks. Mature but benefiting from 5G and fiber buildout.

What distinguishes Lumentum from a generic cyclical recovery is the structural nature of the demand driver. The transition from electrical to optical interconnects inside data centers is governed by physics, not market cycles. Above 400 Gbps, copper cables hit signal integrity limits that no amount of engineering can overcome. As AI clusters scale from hundreds to tens of thousands of GPUs — each requiring multiple optical links at 800G or higher — the total addressable market for optical transceivers expands exponentially. Lumentum's vertical integration across lasers, receivers, and complete transceiver modules positions it to capture this demand at every layer of the stack.
