Investment Thesis
The Largest Nuclear Fleet in America — Powering the AI Revolution. Constellation Energy operates 21 nuclear reactors across 12 plants — the largest nuclear fleet in the United States and the single largest source of carbon-free electricity in the country. 1  |  PRICE ACTION & REVENUE TRAJECTORY

CEG peaked near $350 in late 2025 before pulling back to $279 amid broader market weakness and the Iran conflict’s impact on energy sector sentiment. Ironically, the Iran conflict should be bullish for nuclear — it highlights energy security risks and accelerates the case for domestic nuclear power. Revenue is lumpy quarter-to-quarter (typical for utilities with seasonal demand patterns), but the earnings trajectory is the real story: FY26E EPS of $11.92 growing to $13.98 in FY27.

The 20% pullback from highs has created a compelling entry point — the stock now trades at a 50% discount to analyst consensus, the widest gap in our coverage universe alongside RKLB. This is the type of dislocation where the macro narrative (war, oil, rates) overwhelms the fundamental story (nuclear renaissance, AI power demand, carbon-free baseload).

2  |  BUSINESS MODEL & COMPETITIVE POSITION
Revenue Architecture
Generation (~70%): Nuclear, natural gas, hydro, wind, and solar generation across 32,000+ MW of capacity. Nuclear provides ~19 GW of baseload power — carbon-free, 24/7, weather-independent.
Customer Supply (~30%): Retail and wholesale electricity supply to commercial, industrial, and government customers. Growing as data center operators seek direct PPAs with nuclear generators.
Why Nuclear Is the AI Power Source of Choice

AI data centers require three things from their power source: reliability (24/7 uptime), scale (hundreds of MW per facility), and carbon-free generation (ESG commitments). Nuclear is the only energy source that delivers all three simultaneously. Solar and wind are intermittent. Natural gas emits carbon. Hydro is geography-constrained. Nuclear runs at 90%+ capacity factor, generates zero carbon, and can scale to GW levels. Constellation’s 21 reactors represent the single largest concentration of this ideal power source in the Western hemisphere.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR MIND

We would downgrade from BUY if:

Nuclear plant utilization falls below 85%. Would signal operational issues that undermine baseload reliability.
Electricity prices decline 20%+ from current levels. Would compress margins and reduce the premium on nuclear generation.
TMI restart is cancelled or delayed beyond 2028. Would undermine the nuclear-AI thesis with the highest-profile deal.
Regulatory action restricts nuclear-to-data-center PPAs. Would limit the highest-growth revenue pathway.
