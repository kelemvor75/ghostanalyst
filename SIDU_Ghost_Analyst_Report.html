Investment Thesis
Revenue Is Declining, Not Growing — The Thesis Has No Foundation. Sidus Space’s revenue has declined from $5.96M (FY2023) to $4.67M (FY2024) to $3.4M (FY2025) — a 43% cumulative decline over two years. This is not a pre-revenue company building toward monetization; it is a company that had revenue and is losing it. The fundamental gate of our model requires positive YoY revenue growth. SIDU fails decisively.
Negative Gross Margins Mean Every Dollar of Revenue Destroys Value. FY2025 gross margin was -31.4%. Cost of revenue ($9.1M) exceeded total revenue ($3.4M) by nearly 3x. This means Sidus Space loses money on every unit of product it delivers before a single dollar of operating expense is spent. This is not a scale problem — it is a business model problem.
Serial Dilution Is the Actual Business Model. Sidus Space closed a 10.8M share offering at $1.50 in December 2025 and launched an ATM (at-the-market) equity program in February 2026 for additional dilution. Cash increased to $43.2M — not from revenue, but from selling stock. The company has raised more capital from equity markets than it has ever generated in revenue. When a company’s primary source of cash is selling shares, shareholders are the product, not the customer.

1  |  PRICE ACTION & REVENUE TRAJECTORY

SIDU has been a 94.9% loss for investors over 3 years. The stock bounced from $0.63 to $5.39 on speculative momentum before collapsing back to $2.10. This is the classic pattern of a micro-cap stock that trades on narrative and retail speculation rather than fundamentals. Volume of 20M shares daily on a 35M share float means the stock turns over its entire float every two days — this is not investing, it is day trading.

The revenue chart tells the story the stock price obscures. Quarterly revenue has been flat to declining: $1.9M → $0.8M → $0.2M → $1.3M → $1.3M. The $0.2M quarter (Q1 2025) represents near-zero commercial activity for a company valued at $211M. There is no revenue trajectory that justifies the current market cap.

2  |  BUSINESS MODEL & COMPETITIVE POSITION
Revenue Architecture
Space Services (~60%): Satellite design and manufacturing (LizzieSat platform), mission operations, technology hosting. Revenue is project-based and unpredictable. LizzieSat-3 is in orbit for commissioning; LizzieSat-4 launch planned for late 2026.
Space & Defense Manufacturing (~30%): Precision machining, 3D printing, mechanical/electrical assembly for space hardware. Contract manufacturing for other space companies. Small, lumpy.
AI/Data Services (~10%): Orlaith AI ecosystem, FeatherEdge processor, Cielo AI solutions. Aspirational — generating minimal revenue. The company describes this as ‘Data-as-a-Service’ but has not demonstrated commercial demand at any meaningful scale.

The fundamental problem with Sidus Space is the gap between narrative and numbers. The company describes itself as a ‘Space Infrastructure-as-a-Service’ provider with AI-enabled satellite data solutions. The reality is $3.4M in annual revenue — less than a single McDonald’s franchise — for a company valued at $211M. The LizzieSat platform has demonstrated on-orbit capability (LizzieSat-3 captured sub-5-meter resolution imagery), but demonstrating technology and monetizing it are entirely different problems. The company has not shown it can convert technology demonstrations into recurring revenue.

The comparison with Rocket Lab (RKLB) is illuminating. RKLB generates $720M in annual revenue, has 16+ launches per year of proven history, and trades at ~$30B. Sidus Space generates $3.4M in revenue, has 1 satellite in orbit (commissioning), and trades at $211M. RKLB’s price-to-sales is ~38x; SIDU’s is ~62x. Sidus Space is more expensive on a revenue basis than Rocket Lab while generating 0.5% of the revenue. This valuation can only be justified by speculation on future contracts that have not materialized.

10  |  PRICE TARGET & CONCLUSION
Price Target: $1.00  |  Rating: SELL

Our $1.00 target represents 52% downside from $2.10. This is Ghost Analyst’s first SELL rating and it is issued with high conviction.

Sidus Space fails every test in our framework. Revenue is declining (-28% YoY). Gross margins are negative (-31%). The net loss is widening (-$29.5M). The company is funding operations through serial equity dilution, not revenue. The 10-K filing has been delayed. Short interest is 13.2% and rising. There is no institutional analyst coverage. The $211M market cap represents a 62x price-to-sales multiple on declining revenue — more expensive than Rocket Lab on a revenue basis while generating 0.5% of RKLB’s revenue.

The LizzieSat technology is real — the satellite is in orbit and capturing imagery. But technology without revenue is a science project, not a business. Until Sidus Space demonstrates the ability to convert its space capabilities into meaningful, growing, positive-margin revenue, the stock is a speculation on narrative, not an investment in fundamentals. At $2.10, even our bull case of $3.00 requires 20x revenue on $12M of revenue that doesn’t exist yet. The bear case of $0.30 is what happens when the ATM dilution continues and the meme cycle ends.
