Decoder

How to read this page

Flow and gamma

Market tide sums every option trade by whether it filled at the ask (buyer aggressive) or the bid (seller aggressive). Net call premium falling while the index rises means calls are being sold into strength: hedged or overwritten, not chased.

Gamma flip is the price where dealers' net gamma changes sign. Above it their hedging leans against the move (quiet, mean-reverting tape); below it their hedging pushes with the move (trend, air pockets). Walls are the strikes with the most positive gamma above and below spot: the first places a move stalls.

Vol and skew

IV rank puts today's implied vol on a 0–100 scale against the last year. Under 20, options are cheap relative to the year and the market is not paying for protection; over 60 it is. Equity ranks near the floor with bond ranks near the ceiling is the signature of a rates-driven tape.

Premium skew is bullish minus bearish premium as a share of the total: +100 all calls bought/puts sold, −100 the reverse. It is a one-day read; pair it with the volume multiple to tell a real bet from noise.

Nothing here is a forecast. It is where the market is positioned, which is what determines how it reacts.
